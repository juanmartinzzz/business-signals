Market roast 5

envelope

Users × price

Side bar is $15–25k USD net take-home, so gross roughly $20–35K after fees, costs, and company tax. At $10/mo ($120/yr) that is ~170–290 paying users retained year-round. Primary bar is $150K net, so ~$200K+ gross solo, which is ~1,700+ paying users at $120/yr. At a typical 2–5% free-to-paid conversion, primary needs roughly 35,000–85,000 free users acquired, retained, and served at near-zero revenue. Against a 50M-user incumbent giving the core product away free, that funnel is bought one expensive click at a time from an audience whose willingness to pay rounds to zero.

capital

Before it pays

$5–15k USD plus 200–400 hours before the first dollar, assuming solo build and no paid ads. Page builder, templates, custom domains, analytics, billing, commerce checkout, and abuse/spam handling — the category's table stakes, all rebuildable in weeks and all worthless without distribution. The real spend is CAC: every user you acquire pays $0 for months (if ever) while support load scales with the free base. This is a business where the product is cheap and every customer loses money until the 3% who sell something upgrade.

K1

A free commodity owned by a 50M-user incumbent

Linktree has 50M+ users, Later ~7M, Beacons 2M+, plus ~20 funded clones. Linktree held ~88% share back at 12M users and the category has been "disrupted" annually since 2016 without denting it. The core product is free everywhere, switching costs the user real pain (the URL is printed in old videos, pins, and stories), and your CAC to steal a user who pays $0 is more than $0. There is no pricing umbrella to hide under: the umbrella is free.

K2

Automations are the anti-value-prop

Link-in-bio wins because it is brain-dead simple: link works, looks cute, takes money. Nobody has ever churned from Linktree for lack of a Zapier canvas. Automations mean setup flows, debugging, broken OAuth, and support tickets — complexity injected into the one product whose entire job is to be dumber than a website. This twist makes the product harder to adopt, harder to support, and no easier to sell.

K3

No payer, no switching trigger

Who pays is unstated — the pitch says nothing about the category business model, which is freemium subscription plus commerce take-rate on the ~3% who sell. Switching bio links is genuinely painful (the link lives everywhere), so dislodging requires a 10x reason like "you'll make $500 more this month." "Now with automations" is a 0.5x reason. No payer plus no trigger equals traffic and $12 in MRR.

K4

The integration treadmill plus platform risk

Each integration is OAuth breakage, rate limits, policy changes, and angry users when TikTok or Meta moves something. The whole acquisition channel rents space on platforms that actively dislike middlemen — one policy update can nuke distribution overnight. A solo founder signing up to maintain N integrations against two hostile giants is signing up for pager duty that pays in free-tier gratitude.

K5

Creators don't pay; the take-rate needs volume you can't buy

The only proven revenue in this space is subscription from power users plus a slice of commerce. Creators churn the month you ask for $10; the payers are the thin slice selling digital products, who already have checkout on Stan/Beacons/Linktree with audiences attached. Capturing enough commerce volume to live on 9–12% of it requires the free-user mountain from the envelope math, which requires the CAC budget this project doesn't have.

Constraints overlay 7

constraint

location.md

Mostly neutral: an English-first remote product asks nothing of Austin. Turns negative the moment a variant needs a physical presence — on-site onboarding, local foot traffic, "come by the shop" — which this file rules out unless that part is someone else's job.

constraint

scale.md

The consumer route violates the solo-or-plus-one cap by stealth: a free-user mountain needs support, abuse handling, and integration maintenance that never ends. There is no solo-sized version of "out-Linktree Linktree."

constraint

time-and-income.md

Side mode dies on upkeep: integrations rot, free users file tickets, uptime is 24/7 for $0/month accounts — the opposite of minimal ongoing effort. Primary mode dies on the envelope: ~1,700 retained payers or ~35–85K free users, neither compatible with sustainable solo economics.

constraint

skills.md

Does not kill. Backend plus PM chops could genuinely build this in weeks; the corpse is not technical. Noted honestly so nobody mistakes "I can build it" for "it can live."

constraint

languages.md

English-first creator tools face the most crowded SEO and paid-acquisition battlefield in this space, and conversational Spanish is not strong enough to open a second front — no language edge anywhere in this plan.

constraint

gaps.md

No gap answers invented. Hours, distribution appetite, sales willingness, and network assets are unknown — but no plausible answer outspends a 50M-user incumbent giving the product away, so the gaps do not rescue the idea either way.

constraint

already-tried.md

One entry (print-on-demand posters, 2022) — not resurrected below. Alternatives are fresh, not recycled.

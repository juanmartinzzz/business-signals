# Time & Income Expectations

(Fictional example — rewrite with your own facts.)

I have a full-time job in tech and want any side activity to remain compatible
with it unless it becomes my primary focus.
Two viable modes only:

All income targets below are net take-home salary to the individual, after all
business expenses, payment fees, and company-level taxes. Gross revenue must be
much higher than the salary numbers. Personal income tax is excluded: the
individual pays that on top of the stated salary.

Side / supplemental income
Time required: very small portion of my time (low ongoing effort after initial setup).
Target: ~$15k–$25k USD per year net take-home to me is acceptable and still
worthwhile, provided the time cost stays minimal.

Primary / full-time focus
Time required: can take essentially all of my professional time.
Target: around $150k USD per year as my base salary, net of all company costs
and company tax, before my personal tax.

If the model involves a part-time helper:

Assume ~$25k USD/year net take-home for them. In a solo-plus-helper setup, the
model must support ~$175k in take-home alone (primary mode), plus company costs
and company tax on top.

When a roast does envelope math, gross it up: salaries plus business expenses,
fees, and company tax, not just the salary total.

The business must be sustainable at the chosen scale (no dependence on
continuous aggressive growth, constant fundraising, or ever-expanding
headcount).

# Potential winner ideas

Keeper files from [`skills/potential-winner-ideas/`](../skills/potential-winner-ideas/) — alternatives from a roast worth thinking through later, or brand-new sparks. Always a **new** file, then a sibling `.html` via `scripts/to-html.py` (never by hand, never overwritten).

```
YYYY-MM-DD---pwi---<slug>.md
YYYY-MM-DD---pwi---<slug>.html
```

`status` lifecycle: `parked` → `testing` → `graduated` or `killed`. Only you
move it; append a `Log` line on every move.

Your PWIs live here at the top level and are **gitignored** — they stay on your
machine. [`samples/`](samples/) holds one committed example (fictional) so you
can see the shape before promoting anything.

# Research

Do not vibe the market. Search. If you cannot open it (paywall, login), skip or mark **unverified**. Do not invent the page.

## Hunts (every run)

1. **Who already sells this**, including boring defaults: Excel, agencies, WhatsApp, the accountant, the intern, the PMS, the bank, Craigslist, the Facebook group.
2. **Where the buyer already looks** (required, at least one search). Not “competitors for X.” Where they hang out, what they google, which communities, which incumbents they already pay.
3. **Failure write-ups**: shutdown posts, “we tried X,” post-mortems.
4. **Pricing and payment**: does anyone pay; list prices; rough unit-econ. Users × price (or hours × rate) against both income modes in `constraints-and-preferences/time-and-income.md`.
5. **Capital before it pays**: tools, ads, inventory, licenses, a contractor, float. A number or a range, with what you assumed. Not a budget they must pre-commit.
6. **Distribution and CAC clues**: can a 1–3 person shop reach the buyer without a sales team.
7. **Regulation, licensing, language, geography** when relevant. A sales or regulatory process in a language you do not speak is friction until `constraints-and-preferences/location.md` says otherwise.
8. **Analogues** in adjacent niches that already won or died.
9. **Competitor health** (required, at least one check). For each named competitor: thriving / struggling / shutdown / unverified. Look for layoffs, shutdown posts, stale funding, stalled changelogs, bad reviews, traffic collapse. The thriving feed `Kings of the hill`; the struggling and dead feed `Obituaries: Respect for the Fallen`.

## Evidence

| Claim type | Bar |
|---|---|
| Named competitor, price, shutdown, struggle signal, regulation | URL |
| “Nobody pays for this” | Search plus what you found (including absence), marked if thin |
| Back-of-envelope | Show the arithmetic; label assumptions |
| Constraints overlay | Cite `constraints-and-preferences/<file>.md`, not a vibe or a generic founder default |

## Kill mechanisms (use these; do not stop at mood)

- Feature, not a product (bundled inside something they already pay for)
- Vitamin, not a painkiller (chronic mild annoyance; they cope)
- Distribution desert (you cannot reach the buyer without a team or a closed local circuit)
- Support swamp (the business becomes inbox and custom work)
- Incumbent is free and good enough
- Only works as a local services business in a language you do not have
- Unit econ needs hundreds of $15/mo users for a “nice to have”
- Needs fundraising, headcount, or aggressive growth to make the primary-income bar
- You would have to become someone else (closer, enterprise account exec, clinic operator)

## Cheap-test research

When naming a cheap test for an alternative, prefer something that produces **money or a hard no** in ≤ 48 hours, else ≤ 1 week. Ask about last time they had the problem and what they already paid — not “would you use this.” Three strangers paying beats ten friends being polite.

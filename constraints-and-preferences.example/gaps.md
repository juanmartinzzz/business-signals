# Gaps

(Fictional example — rewrite with your own facts.)

Unanswered on purpose. The roast skill must not invent answers, and must not
fill them with generic “want a big company” defaults. Fill later if you want a
sharper overlay.

- **Hours.** “Very small portion of time” is the whole game for side mode. A
  number (e.g. 3–5 h/week after setup vs 10–15) would change which ideas survive.
- **Distribution appetite.** Content, outbound, paid, community, product-led,
  personal network (ex-coworkers, Austin tech, Twitter).
- **Sales willingness.** Founder-led sales on calls? Async only?
- **Anti-goals / already rejected categories.** Separate from
  `already-tried.md`: things you will not do even as an alternative.
- **Risk / visibility.** Side project vs day job; brands you will not attach
  your name to.
- **Network assets.** Who you can reach without paid acquisition.

Capital required before an idea pays is **not** a gap here. Each roast estimates
that as a research result. Do not pre-commit a budget.

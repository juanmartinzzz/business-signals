# roast: ai-notes-for-dentists

## Sources
- https://www.ada.org — clinics already live inside practice software
- unverified: $15/mo dental SaaS conversion is “nice to have”

## Target
Sell dentists a $15/month app that writes their chart notes from the appointment. They switch because notes are tedious. Working means enough paying clinics for side income, or a lot more for primary.

## Market roast

### Envelope
At $15/mo you need about 110–195 paying clinics for $15–25k USD. That is a lot of dentists who already have notes inside their PMS. Primary ($150k) is a fantasy at this price.

### Capital before it pays
A few hundred in tools. The real spend is the months of selling into clinics before anyone pays.

### K1 — Bundled in the PMS
Dentists already get notes inside the software they cannot leave. You are a second window they will not open.

### K2 — Vitamin
The pain is real and mild. They cope with templates and a bored associate. Nobody is dying for this. O1 already proved it.

## Kings of the hill

### H1 — Big PMS Inc
- What they do: practice software with chart notes bundled in
- Moat: they own the default; see K1
- Lesson: you are a second window they will not open

## Obituaries: Respect for the Fallen

### O1 — DeadNotes
- What they did: standalone AI chart notes for dentists
- Status: dead
- Cause of death: unverified: shutdown post rumored, site dead
- Lesson: standalone notes already died once; bundling won

## Constraints overlay

### C1 — location.md
Irrelevant if you sell English-first and remote. Do not twist this into on-site clinic software.

### C2 — time-and-income.md
Side income is a stretch. Primary is a joke. Selling into clinics is not “low ongoing effort.”

### C3 — skills.md
You can build it in a weekend. That is the trap.

## Alternatives

### A1 — Weekly notes retainer
- Change: stop selling software; clean last week’s notes by hand for English-speaking clinic managers, billed as a retainer
- Why it might live: they already pay humans for admin; a retainer is a known object
- How it still dies: it becomes a tiny agency and eats evenings
- Mode: side
- Fit: skills.md; languages.md; scale.md
- Cheap test: this week message 15 clinic managers (not friends); offer to clean last week’s notes for $150 by Friday; kill if fewer than 2 pay; likes do not count

### A2 — Notes API for PMS builders
- Change: sell a capture-and-return API to people building clinic tools, not to dentists
- Why it might live: fewer buyers, higher price, your builder skills actually matter
- How it still dies: sales cycle; you still have to find those builders
- Mode: primary
- Fit: skills.md; scale.md
- Cheap test: list a $500 setup + monthly on a forum those builders actually read; kill if zero paid conversations in 7 days

## Verdict
- Main: kill
- Side: only as A1, and only after the cheap test pays
- Primary: do not do this
